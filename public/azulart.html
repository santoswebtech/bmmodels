Travel Information

GLOBAL WORLDWIDE TOUR

Travel, Flights & Passport Information

Blue Media Enterprises is committed to helping every participant have a smooth and organized international travel experience. However, participants are responsible for securing their own international airfare and ensuring that they have valid travel documents, including a valid passport.

INTERNATIONAL FLIGHTS

Blue Media Enterprises does not cover or purchase international flights on behalf of participants. Airfare is separate from the Global Worldwide Tour participation fee and must be arranged and paid for by each traveler.

Flight prices vary depending on the destination, airline, travel dates, availability, and how far in advance the ticket is purchased. As a general example, round-trip flights from the United States to Europe may sometimes be found for approximately $380–$500 round trip when booked early, with other fares commonly ranging around $500–$700 or more, depending on the dates and market conditions.

Because airfare prices change frequently, participants are encouraged to book as early as possible to take advantage of better rates.

Travel Documents

PASSPORT & TRAVEL DOCUMENTS

Each participant is responsible for obtaining and maintaining their own passport and any other travel documents required for international travel.

Blue Media Enterprises is not responsible for obtaining, renewing, replacing, or paying for a participant’s passport.

However, our team can assist and point participants in the right direction regarding the passport application or renewal process and provide general guidance on where to obtain the appropriate information and forms.

Participants should make sure their passport is valid for the required period before making international travel arrangements.

Global Takeover

WHAT THE TOUR FEE COVERS

The Global Worldwide Tour offers Basic and Exclusive participation options, allowing participants to choose the level of accommodations, transportation, access, and content opportunities that best fit their travel experience.

BASIC TOUR PACKAGE

The Basic Package provides participants with the opportunity to fully experience the Global Worldwide Tour and participate in the scheduled activities, experiences, networking opportunities, and entertainment.

The Basic Package includes:

  • Regular bedroom accommodations
  • Shared restroom/bathroom accommodations
  • Access to scheduled tour activities and experiences
  • Participation in the group tour experience
  • Attendance at organized fashion, entertainment, networking, nightlife, and cultural activities included in the tour
  • Transportation primarily through public transportation and/or Uber, depending on the location and activity
  • Professional photography opportunities, based on the tour schedule and available photo sessions
  • Content creation opportunities, with more limited on-camera/content-production opportunities compared with the Exclusive Package
  • Access to the overall Global Worldwide Tour experience and group activities
  • Additional tour-related activities outlined in the participant’s selected package

The Basic Package is designed for participants who want to travel with the group, experience the destination, attend the activities, network, and be part of the Global Worldwide Tour at an accessible level.

EXCLUSIVE TOUR PACKAGE

The Exclusive Package provides an enhanced and more personalized Global Worldwide Tour experience, with upgraded accommodations, transportation, VIP access, and increased opportunities for photography and content creation.

The Exclusive Package includes:

  • Master bedroom accommodations
  • Private restroom/bathroom
  • Access to all scheduled tour activities and experiences
  • Participation in the full Global Worldwide Tour experience
  • Priority VIP transportation/rides to select locations and activities, when available and applicable
  • Enhanced transportation arrangements compared with the Basic Package
  • Increased opportunities for professional photo shoots
  • Increased on-camera content creation opportunities
  • Additional media and content opportunities throughout the tour
  • Fashion, entertainment, networking, nightlife, and cultural experiences included in the tour
  • Additional tour-related activities outlined in the participant’s selected package

The Exclusive Package is designed for participants who want a more private, comfortable, VIP, and content-focused experience while traveling with Blue Media Enterprises.
