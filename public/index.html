CEO Profile

Leadership built around preparation, visibility, and opportunity.

Ijahlon Fields is the Founder and CEO of Blue Media Enterprises. He is a media executive, entrepreneur, author, creator, and mentor whose work centers on branding, media development, talent development, and business growth. His leadership approach emphasizes professionalism, preparation, consistency, visibility, and helping clients build stronger careers and brands through real-world opportunities and strategic relationships.

Fields brings experience in entrepreneurship, leadership, media development, mentorship, entertainment, fashion, production, and business. His background includes work as an author and creator, including Shadows of Ezra and the film project Mr. Time Traveler, along with a foundation in International Business.

Blue Media Mission

The goal is not simply to book an appearance but to help talent improve presentation, build professional assets, gain credible exposure, and enter stronger rooms with better preparation.

Model Development Approach

A structured path from talent potential to professional visibility.

1

Build the brand

Strengthen presentation, image, positioning, and professional materials to create a sharper personal brand.

2

Create the assets

Develop comp cards, headshots, EPKs, photography, and video content that support model discovery and industry credibility.

3

Develop the talent

Provide runway preparation, coaching resources, consultations, and professional feedback to sharpen confidence and execution.

4

Create visibility

Pursue runway, media, interview, magazine, advertising, and content opportunities that expand professional reach.

5

Expand the network

Facilitate strategic introductions to agencies, brands, designers, media professionals, and other relevant industry resources.

6

Go global

Position qualified models for domestic and international opportunities when aligned with the model and the opportunity.
